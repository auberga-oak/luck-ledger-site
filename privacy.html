Privacy

Who is responsible. Finansium AB is the data controller for Luck Habits. Contact: henrik.ekenberg@finansium.se.

What we collect. Only your email address, when you sign up.

Why. To send you the Luck Habits newsletter once a week. The legal basis is your consent, which you give when you sign up and confirm your address.

Who processes it. Kit (kit.com) stores your address and sends the emails for us, as our data processor. We don't sell or share your address with anyone else.

Page views. We count page views with GoatCounter, which sets no cookies and stores no personal data, so we only see totals such as how many times a page was read and which site sent the visitor.

How long. Until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it.

Your rights. You can unsubscribe at any time with the link in every email. You can also ask to see, correct or delete your data by emailing the address above. If you think we've handled your data wrongly, you can complain to the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY), imy.se.
