Thanks for signing up. Here's what you'll get.

Every Sunday I'll send one true story about someone who got lucky. I link the source so you can check it. Then I name the move behind it, one of the 33 moves in *A larger bucket*, and give you one small thing to try that week. It should take about ten minutes.

I started collecting these because I kept noticing that lucky people were rarely just lucky. Usually they'd been standing somewhere useful for a long time, or they knew enough to spot what everyone else walked past.

The first story is about a $3 jacket from a Goodwill bin in Portland. It arrives next Sunday.

If you have a luck story of your own, or one you've read about, reply and tell me. I read every reply.
